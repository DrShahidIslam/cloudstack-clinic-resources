Dental Resources Library

Welcome to the reference library of Dr Shahid Islam Dental Clinic. This site collects the practical information our patients ask for most: side-by-side treatment comparisons, an honest explanation of how dental treatment costs are worked out, checklists for your first visit, and aftercare notes you can return to once you are home. Everything here is written in plain language and checked against how the clinic actually works.

Most people arrive at this library with one of three questions. They want to know which treatment suits their tooth, what a fair quote should include, or how to look after dental work once it is done. If you are comparing options for a painful or damaged tooth, a good place to begin is the clinic's own page on root canal treatment in Karachi, then come back here and read the comparison tables with your own situation in mind. The library explains the background; your treatment decision itself is always made after an examination at the clinic.

Treatments in brief

The library supports the treatments the clinic provides every week. The short notes below give the plain summary; the linked pages on the main site carry the full detail.

Treatments patients compare most often
TreatmentIn plain wordsRead more
Root canal treatment The infected inside of a tooth is cleaned out and sealed, so the natural tooth is kept instead of removed. Root canal treatment
Dental implants A titanium post placed in the jaw acts as an artificial root, carrying a crown that looks and works like a tooth. Dental implants
Braces and aligners Teeth are moved gradually into better positions, either with fixed braces or with removable clear aligners. Orthodontics and braces
Teeth whitening Professional whitening lifts surface and deeper stains from the enamel for a brighter, even colour. Teeth whitening

How to use these guides

Read the page that matches your question before your appointment, and write down anything you want to ask. Bring the list with you. Our reference FAQs answer the questions that come up again and again, including why the clinic does not publish a price list and why an X-ray is often the first step. Remember that every mouth is different: a table can compare treatments in general, but only an examination, usually with an X-ray, shows which option is right for your tooth. Where a guide and your dentist's advice differ, follow your dentist.
