Dental Treatment FAQs in Karachi

Short, straight answers to the questions patients ask this clinic most often. Each answer is kept brief on purpose; where a subject deserves more room, the answer links to the library page or clinic page that covers it in full. If your question is not here, the clinic team can be reached on +92 344 212 3913.

Does the clinic publish a price list?

No. The clinic quotes after examining the tooth, because the real cost depends on what the examination and X-ray find: which tooth, how many root canals, the level of infection, and the materials needed. A firm figure is given in writing before treatment starts. Our page on how dental costs are quoted shows how a quote is built, part by part.

Why do I need an X-ray before treatment?

Because most of what decides your treatment is hidden: decay between teeth, infection at the tip of a root, root shape and bone levels cannot be seen by eye. An X-ray lets the dentist diagnose properly and quote honestly, instead of discovering problems halfway through treatment. The dental X-ray guide explains each type of image.

Is it better to save a tooth with a root canal or to remove it?

Saving your natural tooth is usually the better option when the tooth has enough sound structure left and the roots are healthy, because nothing bites quite like your own tooth. Removal and replacement with an implant is the better choice when the tooth is cracked below the gum, too broken down to rebuild, or a previous root canal has failed. The comparison tables set the two paths side by side.

How long does a dental implant take from start to finish?

In most cases the full process runs over several months, because the implant needs time to fuse with the jawbone before it can carry the final crown. The stages are placement, healing and review, then the crown. In suitable cases, same-day implant options shorten the early stages; your dentist will tell you after assessing your bone whether you are a candidate.

What is the difference between braces and clear aligners?

Braces are fixed to the teeth and work all day without any effort from you, and they can handle the widest range of problems. Clear aligners are removable transparent trays that are hard to notice, but they only work while worn, around 22 hours a day, so they suit motivated patients with mild to moderate crowding or spacing.

Which crown material is the strongest?

Zirconia is the strongest of the three common crown materials and is usually chosen for back teeth and heavy bites. E-max is not quite as strong but looks the most like natural enamel, so it is often chosen for front teeth. Metal-ceramic crowns have a metal base with porcelain over it and remain a serviceable, often cheaper option. The crown materials comparison has the full table.

How should I clean and store my dentures?

Clean them daily with a soft brush and denture cleanser or mild soap, never gritty toothpaste, and always over water or a towel so a drop does not crack them. At night, take them out and soak them in clean water or a denture solution so they stay moist and keep their shape. Never use hot water, which can warp the fit. The dentures care guide gives the full routine.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring your ID, any previous dental records or X-rays you have, a list of the medicines you take, and a note of your allergies and symptoms, including when the problem started and what triggers it. Written questions help too. Our first visit checklist covers each item in detail.
