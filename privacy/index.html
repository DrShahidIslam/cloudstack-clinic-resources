Privacy

Privacy and Disclosure

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you read the Dental Resources Library, and who is behind the site. The short version: this is a static reading site with no accounts, no forms and no comment boxes, so it collects almost nothing.

Information this site collects

The library does not ask you to register, sign in or fill in any form, and it has no comment facility. There is nowhere on these pages to type your name, phone number or health details, and we ask you not to send medical information through general email either: appointment enquiries belong with the clinic, by phone, WhatsApp or its own website. Like almost every website, the hosting service keeps standard server logs, which record pages requested, times and the type of browser used. Those logs are used to keep the site working and to understand which guides are read, and they are not sold or shared for advertising.

Cookies and the embedded map

The library pages themselves set no advertising or tracking cookies. One page, the contact page, embeds a Google map so you can find the clinic. That map is served by Google, and Google may set its own cookies and process your visit under its own privacy policy when the map loads. If you prefer, you can find the clinic by its written address instead: Office #102, Plot #12-C, 35th Commercial Street, Tauheed Commercial Area, DHA Phase 5, Karachi.

Links to other websites

This library links to the clinic's main website at drshahidislam.com, to the clinic's three companion sites in the same network, and to services such as WhatsApp and Google Maps when you choose to open them. Once you follow a link, the site you arrive at is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one. The clinic's main website describes its own handling of appointment enquiries there.

Disclosure

The Dental Resources Library is published for Dr Shahid Islam Dental Clinic as a patient education project. The links from this library to the clinic's website are links to the clinic's own business, published openly, not paid placements, and this library carries no advertising and no sponsored or affiliate links. The guides are educational: they describe treatments in general and are not a diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a dentist who has examined you. Clinic facts published here, the address, phone number, email and hours, are the same facts published on the clinic's official website.

Questions about privacy

If you have a question about this page or about how the library handles information, write to the clinic at appointments@drshahidislam.com, or call +92 344 212 3913 during clinic hours: Monday to Thursday 4:30 PM to 9:00 PM, and Saturday 1:30 PM to 5:00 PM.
