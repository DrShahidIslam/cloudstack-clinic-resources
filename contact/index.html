Booking through the main website

The clinic's own website carries the full booking route, along with its treatment pages and cost guides. Use the contact page on drshahidislam.com to request an appointment, or start from the official clinic website if you want to read about a treatment first. If you have already read a guide here, mention it when you call: it helps the team know what you have understood and what you still want explained. If you prefer to be treated by a female dentist, ask for Dr Vinessa Gill when you book.

What to say when you call

When you ring, the team will ask for your name, a contact number, and a short description of the problem: which tooth, when it started, and whether there is pain, swelling or a broken tooth. That short conversation decides whether you need a routine slot or the soonest urgent one, so it is worth having the details ready. You will be offered the next suitable appointment in clinic hours, and most first visits follow the pattern set out in the library's first visit checklist: history, examination, an X-ray where needed, and then a clear explanation of the findings and the cost of the recommended treatment before any work begins. If you need a particular time because of work, say so; as an evening clinic, our Monday to Thursday hours run to 9:00 PM, which suits many patients coming after office hours.

A note about urgent problems

Toothache, a broken tooth, a lost crown or swelling should not wait for a routine slot. Call the clinic number during opening hours and explain that it is urgent; the team will advise you on the soonest way to be seen. The main website's emergency dentist page sets out what counts as an emergency and what to do while you wait. The library's aftercare guides also list the warning signs after treatment that should always prompt a call the same day.
